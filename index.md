## About

I am a Ph.D. student in Operations Research in the H. Milton Stewart School of Industrial and Systems Engineering (ISyE) at Georgia Tech, where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Yao Xie. My research interests lie broadly in statistical learning and sequential analysis, with a particular focus on change-point detection, conformal prediction, and uncertainty quantification. I am interested in developing statistically principled and computationally efficient methods for sequential learning and decision-making under uncertainty.

Prior to Georgia Tech, I received my B.S. in Statistics and Mathematical Sciences from Seoul National University.
